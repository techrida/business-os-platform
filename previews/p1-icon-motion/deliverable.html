    What each motion means

    New notification: the badge springs in from nothing (or its number rolls up). It overshoots and dips before settling. 215 ms after it starts, the bell is kicked into a ringing swing (+11.6°, −7.8°, +5.1°…), with the clapper trailing 50 ms behind. Every value is fitted to the reference video.

    Bell click: the same kick and ring, and the bell fills in while its list is open. Opening never marks anything read; only clicking an item or “Mark all read” does, and the badge then shrinks away or counts down without a swing.

    Keyboard: Enter or Space opens instantly with no swing. Reduced motion: no swing, roll or slide; the badge, fill and drawer fade instead.
