    What each motion means

    New notification: the badge pops in (or its number rolls up), and its landing knocks the bell into a swing that dies away. The clapper trails behind the bell. About 0.8s.

    Bell click: a smaller, quicker swing, and the bell fills in while its list is open. Opening never marks anything read; only clicking an item or “Mark all read” does, and the badge then shrinks away or counts down without a swing.

    Keyboard: Enter or Space opens instantly with no swing. Reduced motion: no swing, roll or slide; the badge, fill and drawer fade instead.
